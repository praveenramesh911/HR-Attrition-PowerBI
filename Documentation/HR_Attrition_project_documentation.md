\# **HR Attrition Analytics Dashboard**



\## 1. Project Overview



The HR Attrition Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition and workforce-related information.



The dashboard provides an overview of employee count, attrition, attrition rate, active employees, age distribution, job satisfaction, education background, department-wise satisfaction, and monthly income across departments.



The project demonstrates the use of Power BI for transforming HR data into interactive visual reports that support workforce analysis.



\---



\## 2. Business Objectives



The main objectives of this project are:



\- Analyze overall employee attrition.

\- Monitor the employee attrition rate.

\- Understand the distribution of active and departed employees.

\- Analyze employees across different age groups.

\- Examine attrition across departments.

\- Analyze attrition by education field.

\- Understand job satisfaction across different job roles.

\- Compare job satisfaction across departments.

\- Analyze average monthly income across departments.

\- Provide an interactive and consolidated view of HR workforce data.



\---



\## 3. Dataset

\## 8. Key Dashboard Observations



Based on the dashboard:



\- The dataset contains 1,470 employees.

\- The dashboard reports 237 employees under attrition.

\- The reported attrition rate is 16.12%.

\- There are 1,233 active employees.

\- The average employee age is 37.

\- Research \& Development has the largest employee count among the departments shown in the department-wise satisfaction visual.

\- Life Sciences and Medical are prominent education fields in the attrition analysis.

\- Job satisfaction varies across different job roles and departments.



These observations are descriptive findings from the dashboard and can be explored further using the underlying employee-level data.
