\# **HR Attrition Analytics – Power BI**



\## 📊 Project Overview



An interactive HR Analytics dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, departments, education fields, and income.



\## 🎯 Business Objectives



\- Analyze employee attrition and attrition rate

\- Monitor active employees

\- Analyze employee distribution by age group

\- Understand attrition across education fields

\- Analyze job satisfaction by job role

\- Compare department-wise job satisfaction

\- Analyze average monthly income by department
